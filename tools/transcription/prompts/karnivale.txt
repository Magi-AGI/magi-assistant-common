This is a tabletop RPG session of "The Ledge: Karnivalé de Fallaçade" (the Carnival), an All Hallows' Eve adventure run for kids. Player characters are orphan Heapsters from the Heap. Players: Ren, Aiden, Wesley, Ezra. Key NPCs and entities: Jean-Pierre (JP), Freddie, Mrs. Shangxi, the Tiger Stove, the Nian, the Noiré, the King of Trouble, the Black Rose, the Troupers, the Puppet Master, Bàrjol. Places and concepts: the Heap, the clocktower, the Witching Hour, the Banishing, Trick-or-Treat, costumes, the carnival, the Raven Clan, the Sauren, the Zoo, the Booger Barn, Copper Corner.
